Questions

What currency will I pay in?

Prices are set in pounds sterling. Checkout shows the price in your own currency, with any sales tax for your country worked out automatically.

How do I get my licence key?

By email, straight after payment. Open the software, go to Help → Licence… (or the Save files step), paste the key and press Apply key.

Which licence do I need?

For your own drives, a Personal licence: 28 days for a single recovery job, or a year if you'd like it on hand. If you recover data for customers — a PC repair shop or IT support — you need the Business licence, which allows commercial use on up to three PCs at one business.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. A licence can be refunded within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. If files the software showed as recoverable come back unusable and support can't put it right, you get a full refund — within 14 days for a 28-day licence, or 30 days for a yearly one. See the refund policy.

Do yearly licences renew automatically?

Yes. We email you before a renewal, and you can cancel at any time. An unwanted renewal is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days. A 28-day licence never renews.

Who sells the licence?

Licences are sold through Onelink (or “Link”), Stripe's merchant-of-record service, which handles payment, tax, receipts and refunds — your receipt will say “Sold through Onelink”. The licence itself is granted by Data Clinic Ltd.
