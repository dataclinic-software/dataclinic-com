Contact support

Questions about a licence, a download, a refund or a scan? Email us and a member of the Data Clinic team will reply, usually within one working day (UK time, Monday to Friday).

Email: customerservices@dataclinic.co.uk

To help us answer quickly, please include the program name and version (shown in the program's Help menu), your Windows version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. If you bought a licence, include the email address you used at checkout.

Before you write

  • Lost your licence key? Tell us the email address you paid with and we'll resend it.
  • Want a refund? See the refund policy for what's covered and how to ask.
  • Drive clicking, not detected, or reported as failing? Software can't fix a physical fault. Stop using the drive. In the UK, our data recovery lab can help.
