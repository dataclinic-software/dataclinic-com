Recover deleted or formatted photos from an SD card

Short answer: Take the card out of the camera or phone straight away — every new photo can overwrite one you're trying to recover. Then connect it to a Windows PC with a card reader and scan it with the free Data Clinic Recovery for Memory Cards: a quick scan for deleted photos, a deep scan for a formatted card.

Do this first

  • Stop using the card. Don't take more photos, and don't format it in the camera.
  • Use a card reader on a Windows 10 or 11 PC. A phone or camera connected by cable often can't be scanned directly.

Recover the photos — free

  1. Download the free Data Clinic Recovery for Memory Cards and install it on your PC (not on the card).
  2. Connect the card. Only removable cards appear — your PC's own drives are never touched.
  3. Quick scan for recently deleted files. It finds any type of file the card's file table still lists, with original names and folders.
  4. Deep scan if the card was formatted or the quick scan comes up short. It recognises 17 formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, TIFF, HEIC, Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, DNG, MP4, MOV, AVI, MP3, WAV, PDF and Word.
  5. Save the photos to your PC — never back onto the card.

Every photo the deep scan finds is checked all the way through. Damaged photos are marked as damaged and shown as they'll actually be saved, so you know what you're getting.

Honest limits

  • Photos that have been overwritten can't be recovered.
  • Deep-scan results come back without their original file names.
  • Videos stored in fragments can come back incomplete.
  • A card that shows 0 MB, isn't recognised or gives constant errors has failed physically — no software can fix that. If many recovered photos are damaged, the card itself may be failing; stop using it.

Questions

Can I recover photos after formatting an SD card?

Often, yes, if you haven't taken new photos since. Run a deep scan.

Is the memory card software really free?

Yes — no trial, no registration and no upsell inside the app.

Can I recover photos from my phone's internal storage?

Not with this software — it works on removable SD and microSD cards. Phone internal storage is encrypted and needs different methods.

Not yet — it runs on Windows 10 and 11.
