About Data Clinic

Data Clinic is a data recovery company, not a software company. Data Clinic Ltd has been recovering data in the United Kingdom since 2002: our engineers work on failed hard drives, SSDs, RAID systems, phones and memory cards every day, in our own lab.

Our software is built from what that work has taught us. That's why it checks a drive's health before it scans, never writes to the drive it's reading, and tells you plainly when a file is gone or a drive needs more than software. We'd rather you knew the truth before you paid than after.

What we make

  • Data Clinic Recovery for Hard Drives — hard drives, SSDs and USB sticks, with Ask Rex AI.
  • Data Clinic Recovery for Memory Cards — free, for SD and microSD cards.
